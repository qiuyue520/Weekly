> **Recording the little things that happen each week**

Monday Same old routine, nothing special, just drifted through the day.

Tuesday Had a meeting in the afternoon, but it was raining cats and dogs during that time; the umbrella was completely useless, and by the time I walked to the classroom, I was basically soaked from head to toe. After the meeting ended and some time had passed in the afternoon, I received a notice saying the teacher would hold a meeting in the studio at noon on Thursday to check our learning progress, and only five people from the studio were called.

Wednesday English class was actually cancelled, but it wasn't much better—I had two classes at 8 a.m. and another two at 9 p.m. Can't wake up early, can't sleep well late, this schedule is absolutely insane... The teacher reminded us again in the WeChat group about the meeting at noon tomorrow.

Thursday No classes the entire day, went for my shift as usual in the morning. At noon, the teacher had something come up and couldn't make it, so it was changed to an online meeting. The five of us who needed to present were all in the studio, so we joined the meeting together. When it was my turn to speak, I suddenly noticed an extra person in the meeting room. The problem was, this person had clearly already left the studio, so technically they had no right to keep mooching off our resources. When I asked about it, I found out that someone saw she wasn't there and actively sent her the meeting code. When someone asked him why he sent it, he actually said: "I saw she was here last time, and I noticed she wasn't here this time, so I sent it to her." Excuse me? The teacher didn't send it to her, so what are you worrying about? If the teacher wanted her in, they would have sent the code privately. Why are you so concerned about her?

Friday Only remembered during morning class that I forgot to do last week's Linux homework. Luckily the teacher didn't scold us, reopened the assignment, and said just submit it before tonight. Then in the evening, I checked and—damn it, I forgot again, and the assignment is already closed...
